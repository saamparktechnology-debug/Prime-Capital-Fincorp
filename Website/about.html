About Us

Trusted Loan Agency in India: Making Financial Freedom Accessible

Founded over a decade ago, Prime Capital Fincorp has grown from a boutique financial advisory firm into one of the country's most trusted independent loan agencies. We offer personal, business and other loans with clear terms, because we believe financial roadblocks shouldn't stop your ambitions.

We believe that accessing financial support should be a clear and informed process. Prime Capital Fincorp is focused on helping customers understand available loan options, compare relevant terms, and make borrowing decisions that suit their individual needs.

Our approach puts customers first, with an emphasis on transparent communication, responsive assistance, and a straightforward loan application experience.

Our mission is to make loan services more accessible by providing clear information, personalized guidance, and support throughout the borrowing journey.

We strive to help customers understand interest rates, repayment terms, applicable charges, and eligibility requirements before making financial commitments.

Our vision is to become a trusted name in customer-focused lending assistance by promoting transparency, responsible borrowing, and convenient access to financial solutions.

We aim to build lasting customer relationships through reliable service, clear communication, and a commitment to informed financial decisions.
